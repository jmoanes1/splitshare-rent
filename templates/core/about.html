{% icon 'sparkles' %} About ShareSplit Rent

Housing that fits real Filipino lives.

We connect people who need an affordable place to stay with homeowners who have a room to share — safely, transparently, and without the usual brokerage runaround.

{% icon 'pin' %} Our mission

Make room-sharing trusted and simple

Millions of Filipinos — students, young professionals, and families — look for a safe, affordable room near work or school. At the same time, countless homes have a spare room sitting empty.

ShareSplit Rent bridges that gap with a marketplace built on verification, honest pricing, and mutual respect. Every listing is reviewed, every user is encouraged to verify their identity, and every booking starts as a request — never a surprise.

Find Your Space. Share More. Live Better. That’s the promise we build toward every day.

{% icon 'lock' %} Trust & safety

How we keep the community safe

Three layers of protection work together on every listing and every request.

{% icon 'user' %}

Identity verification

Renters and hosts upload a government-issued ID. Our admin team reviews each document before granting verified status.

{% icon 'home' %}

Listing approval

Properties pass admin review before publication. Photos, pricing, amenities, and house rules are checked for honesty and completeness.

{% icon 'mail' %}

Request-based booking

Nothing is confirmed automatically. Hosts read each request, ask questions, and accept only when both sides are ready.
