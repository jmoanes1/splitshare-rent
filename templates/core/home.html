{% icon 'sparkles' %} How it works

Three simple steps to your next home

From search to move-in, ShareSplit Rent keeps every step transparent and secure.

1

Create your free account

Sign up as a renter or host and verify your identity with a government ID. Verification builds trust for everyone in the community.

2

Find or list a room

Renters browse approved rooms by city, budget, and room type. Hosts list individual rooms with photos, amenities, and honest monthly pricing.

3

Request and move in

Send a booking request with your preferred move-in date. Once the host accepts, coordinate the details and settle into your new space.
